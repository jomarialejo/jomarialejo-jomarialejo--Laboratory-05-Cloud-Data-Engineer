## 1. Why is object storage better suited for storing millions of photos compared to a traditional block storage hard drive?

## 3. What is a "bucket" in the context of cloud storage?
Key points to cover: a named container for objects, flat namespace (no real folders), access policies applied at the bucket level.

Key points to cover: replication across multiple servers/data centers, redundancy (e.g. erasure coding, RAID-like techniques), automated backups, multi-region storage.
